Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 March 2026  ·  Effective date: 31 March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Master Your Career Path (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses and protects your personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who We Are

Master Your Career Path is a career development brand operated by Didi, trading as Master Your Career Path, based in the United Kingdom.

Data Controller contact:
Email: didi@masteryourcareerpath.com

2. What Personal Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Identity data: your first name
  • Contact data: your email address
  • Profile data: your current career stage (where you tell us via the OPERATE waitlist form)
  • Usage data: information about how you use our website (e.g. pages visited, time on site) — collected via cookies where you have consented
  • Transaction data: if you purchase a paid programme through Skool, payment data is handled entirely by Skool and their payment processor. We do not store card or payment details.

3. How We Collect Your Data

  • OPERATE waitlist form — when you sign up to be notified about the OPERATE programme
  • Skool community — when you join our free or paid community on Skool, their platform collects and processes your data under their own privacy policy
  • Email communications — when you contact us directly at didi@masteryourcareerpath.com
  • Cookies and analytics — with your consent, via our website cookie banner

4. Why We Use Your Data (Lawful Basis)

Under UK GDPR, we rely on the following lawful bases:

  • Consent — for the OPERATE waitlist, email marketing, and non-essential cookies. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Contract — to deliver the services you have purchased (PRIME™ Membership, PRIME™ Cohort, or PRIME™ 1:1 coaching).
  • Legitimate interests — to improve our website and services, respond to enquiries, and maintain security, where this does not override your rights.

5. How We Use Your Data

  • To notify you when OPERATE™ launches (if you have joined the waitlist and consented)
  • To send you relevant career resources, updates and programme information (where you have consented)
  • To deliver and administer any programme or membership you have purchased
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide support
  • To improve our website content and user experience
  • To comply with legal obligations

6. How We Share Your Data

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it only in the following circumstances:

  • Skool — our community platform. When you join our Skool community, Skool processes your data as a data processor on our behalf and independently as a data controller under their own terms. Please review Skool’s Privacy Policy.
  • Email service providers — if we use a third-party email platform to send newsletters or updates, they process your email address on our behalf.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our business or others.

7. Cookies

Our website uses cookies. We distinguish between:

  • Strictly necessary cookies — essential for the site to function. These are set without consent.
  • Analytics cookies — used to understand how visitors use our site. These are only set with your explicit consent via our cookie banner.

You can withdraw cookie consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies or by using the cookie settings option on our site.

8. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Waitlist data — retained until OPERATE launches and for a reasonable period thereafter, or until you ask to be removed.
  • Customer data — retained for 7 years from the end of a contract for legal and accounting purposes.
  • Marketing data — retained until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent.
  • Website usage data — retained in aggregated/anonymised form only after 26 months.

9. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure (“right to be forgotten”) — ask us to delete your data where there is no compelling reason for continued processing
  • Right to restrict processing — ask us to limit how we use your data in certain circumstances
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, commonly used format
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting prior processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at didi@masteryourcareerpath.com. We will respond within one calendar month.

10. International Transfers

Our primary operations are based in the UK. Some of our service providers (e.g. Skool) are based in the United States. Where data is transferred outside the UK, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with UK GDPR requirements.

11. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

12. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your personal data, please contact us first at didi@masteryourcareerpath.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK supervisory authority for data protection:

  • Website: ico.org.uk
  • Helpline: 0303 123 1113

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
